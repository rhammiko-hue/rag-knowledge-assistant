# Freight Audit and Payment

## Invoice Auditing
Every freight invoice is matched against the original rate quote and the bill of lading before payment. An automated tolerance of 2% is allowed for minor accessorial charge differences; anything above 2% is flagged for manual review.

## Common Discrepancies
The most frequent discrepancies are duplicate fuel surcharges, incorrect weight breaks, and detention charges without supporting timestamps. Auditors must resolve flagged invoices within 5 business days.

## Payment Terms
Approved carrier invoices are paid on net-30 terms. Carriers enrolled in the quick-pay program receive payment in 7 days in exchange for a 1.5% discount.

## Carrier Scorecards
Carrier performance is scored monthly on on-time pickup, on-time delivery, invoice accuracy, and claims ratio. Carriers falling below 90% on-time delivery for two consecutive months are placed on probation.
