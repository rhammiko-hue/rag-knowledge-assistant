# Cold Chain Handling Procedures

## Temperature Requirements
Refrigerated goods must be kept between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius throughout storage and transit. Frozen goods must remain at or below minus 18 degrees Celsius. Temperature is monitored continuously by calibrated sensors.

## Pre-Cooling
Trailers must be pre-cooled to the required temperature range before loading begins. Loading dock doors are kept closed except during active pallet transfer to minimize temperature excursions.

## Excursion Response
If a temperature excursion beyond the allowed range lasts more than 30 minutes, the shipment is quarantined and quality assurance is notified. QA determines whether the product can be released, downgraded, or destroyed.

## Documentation
Temperature logs accompany every cold chain shipment. Drivers record trailer temperature at pickup and delivery, and any excursion is documented with timestamps and corrective actions.
