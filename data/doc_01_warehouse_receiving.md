# Warehouse Receiving Procedures

## Inbound Scheduling
All inbound trucks must be scheduled through the dock appointment system at least 24 hours in advance. The receiving dock operates from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Carriers arriving without an appointment are worked in on a first-come, first-served basis only if dock capacity allows.

## Unloading and Inspection
Upon arrival, the driver checks in at the guard shack and proceeds to the assigned dock door. Warehouse staff unload pallets and verify the count against the bill of lading. Any visible damage is photographed and noted on the delivery receipt before the driver departs.

## Put-Away
After inspection, goods are labeled with license plate numbers and moved to their assigned storage locations within 4 hours of unloading. High-velocity SKUs are directed to forward pick locations near the packing stations.

## Discrepancies
Shortages, overages, or damaged goods are logged in the warehouse management system within 2 hours. The purchasing team is notified automatically for any variance above $500.
