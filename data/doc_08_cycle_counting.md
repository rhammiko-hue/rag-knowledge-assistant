# Cycle Counting Procedures

## Counting Frequency
A-class SKUs are cycle counted weekly, B-class SKUs monthly, and C-class SKUs quarterly. High-value items above $1,000 per unit are counted weekly regardless of ABC class.

## Blind Counts
Counters perform blind counts without seeing the system quantity. A second count is required if the first count differs from the system quantity by more than 1% or $100, whichever is greater.

## Reconciliation
Inventory control investigates and reconciles variances within 24 hours. Root causes such as misplaced pallets, unit-of-measure errors, or unrecorded shipments are documented for each adjustment.

## Accuracy Targets
The target inventory record accuracy is 98% for A-class SKUs and 95% overall. Facilities below target must submit a corrective action plan.
