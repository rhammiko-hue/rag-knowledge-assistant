# Customs and Trade Compliance

## Import Documentation
Every US import shipment requires CBP Form 7501 (Entry Summary), a commercial invoice, a packing list, and the bill of lading. The customs broker must receive documents at least 48 hours before vessel arrival.

## Duties and Classification
Products are classified under the Harmonized Tariff Schedule to determine duty rates. The trade compliance team reviews classifications annually and whenever a product design changes.

## Denied Party Screening
All customers, suppliers, and intermediaries are screened against denied party lists before any transaction. Matches are escalated to the compliance officer and the transaction is held.

## Record Keeping
Import and export records are retained for 5 years from the date of entry. The compliance team conducts quarterly self-audits of broker filings and duty payments.
