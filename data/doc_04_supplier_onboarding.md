# Supplier Onboarding

## Required Documentation
New suppliers must submit a completed W-9 (or W-8BEN for foreign entities), a certificate of insurance meeting our minimum coverage limits, banking details for ACH payments, and a signed supplier code of conduct.

## Qualification Process
The sourcing team evaluates suppliers on quality certifications (ISO 9001 preferred), financial stability, production capacity, and references from at least two existing customers. The full qualification process typically takes 3 to 4 weeks.

## System Setup
Once approved, the supplier record is created in the ERP system with payment terms, lead times, and approved ship-from locations. Suppliers gain access to the vendor portal for purchase orders, invoices, and shipment tracking.

## Performance Monitoring
New suppliers are reviewed after the first 90 days. Ongoing scorecards track defect rates, on-time delivery, and responsiveness. Suppliers must maintain a defect rate below 1% to retain preferred status.
