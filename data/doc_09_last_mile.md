# Last-Mile Delivery Operations

## Delivery Promises
Orders in metro areas are eligible for same-day delivery if placed before 2:00 PM. Standard delivery promises are 2 business days for regional zones and 3 to 5 business days for remote zones.

## Driver Procedures
Drivers follow optimized route sequences from the dispatch app and capture proof of delivery with a photo and GPS timestamp. Failed deliveries are re-attempted the next business day; after two failed attempts the package is held at a pickup point.

## Customer Notifications
Customers receive SMS notifications at dispatch, when the driver is 5 stops away, and at delivery. Real-time tracking is available through the order status page.

## Performance Metrics
Key metrics include on-time delivery rate (target 97%), cost per stop, and first-attempt success rate. Routes are re-optimized monthly based on stop density changes.
