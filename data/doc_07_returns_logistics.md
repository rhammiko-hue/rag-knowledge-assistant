# Returns and Reverse Logistics

## Return Window
Customers have 30 days from the delivery date to return unused items in their original packaging. Items returned after 30 days are accepted only with manager approval.

## Inspection and Grading
Returned items are inspected within 48 hours of arrival at the returns center. Products are graded as resellable, refurbishable, or scrap. Resellable items are restocked within 24 hours of grading.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return being graded as resellable. Customers receive email notification when the refund is processed.

## Reverse Logistics Network
The company operates three regional returns centers. Carriers consolidate return shipments weekly to reduce transportation cost per unit.
