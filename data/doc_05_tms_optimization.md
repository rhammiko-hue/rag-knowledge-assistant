# Transportation Management System (TMS) Optimization

## Load Planning
The TMS consolidates less-than-truckload shipments into full truckloads whenever the combined volume exceeds 80% of trailer capacity. Optimized load plans are generated nightly for the next day's shipments.

## Carrier Selection
For each lane, the TMS ranks carriers by total cost, transit time reliability, and capacity commitment. The target on-time pickup rate across all carriers is 98%, and lanes falling below 95% trigger an automatic carrier review.

## Route Optimization
Dynamic routing considers traffic patterns, driver hours-of-service limits, and delivery appointment windows. Multi-stop routes are limited to 5 stops to protect on-time performance.

## Analytics
Weekly dashboards report cost per mile, cost per shipment, empty miles percentage, and tender acceptance rates. The logistics team reviews these metrics every Monday to identify savings opportunities.
